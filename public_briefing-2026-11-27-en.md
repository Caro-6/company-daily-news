# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (November 27, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

- On November 27, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada published a notice extending the temporary border measures in response to the Ebola disease outbreak by a further 90 days, until February 25, 2027 at 23:59:59 Eastern Time.
- While the measures remain in place, immigration documents will continue to be suspended for people outside Canada who listed the Democratic Republic of the Congo, South Sudan or Uganda as their country of residence when they applied, even if their visa, permit or electronic travel authorization was previously approved; the department will continue processing affected applications but will not finalize them while the measures are in place.
- No other official channels reported new changes today.

## Latest Notices and Policy Changes

- November 27, 2026, temporary border measures in response to the Ebola disease outbreak extended again: Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada extended the temporary immigration measures by 90 days, until February 25, 2027 at 23:59:59 Eastern Time
- Who is affected: people outside Canada who listed the Democratic Republic of the Congo, South Sudan or Uganda as their country of residence when they applied for the immigration document
- What the measures do: immigration documents for those people remain suspended, even if the visa, permit or electronic travel authorization was previously approved
- Applications: the department will continue processing new and existing applications affected by these measures, but will not finalize them while the measures are in place
- This is a further extension of the same measures; the previous extension notice was published on September 28, 2026
