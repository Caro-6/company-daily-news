## Latest Announcements and Policy Changes

- Ebola-related temporary border and immigration measures extended (September 28, 2026, IRCC notice)
  - Temporary immigration measures extended by 60 days, until November 27, 2026
  - Immigration documents remain suspended for residents of the Democratic Republic of Congo, Uganda, and South Sudan; people whose last country of residence at the time of application was one of these countries cannot travel to Canada at this time, even with a previously approved visa, permit, or electronic travel authorization
  - IRCC will continue processing affected new and existing applications, but will not finalize them while the measures are in place
