# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (November 16, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

- Newfoundland and Labrador issued 54 invitations on November 16, including 12 under the Provincial Nominee Program and 42 under the Atlantic Immigration Program, bringing the province's 2026 total to 3,381.

## Invitations and Selections

### Newfoundland and Labrador: 54 invitations issued on November 16

- Draw date: November 16, 2026
- Total invitations: 54
- Breakdown: Newfoundland and Labrador Provincial Nominee Program (NLPNP), 12; Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP), 42
- Minimum score: the province does not publish one
- 2026 total: 3,381 invitations across 25 rounds
- November rounds so far: 224 invitations in five rounds — 50 on November 2, 25 on November 4, 57 on November 9, 38 on November 13, and 54 on November 16
- This round had the highest Atlantic Immigration Program share of any November round, with 42 of 54 invitations; an invitation is not a nomination or a permanent residence approval, and invited candidates must submit their applications within the required timeframe

## Announcements and Policy Changes

### IRCC publishes three program delivery updates (November 16, 2026)

- Resettlement: new guidance for officers on how to proceed with a refugee resettlement application when the applicant is in a location where it is not operationally feasible for IRCC to process the application
- Medical assessment coding: updated guidance reflecting the Medical Surveillance Codes (S codes) introduced for the streamlined Immigration Medical Examination, with improved definitions of the medical assessment codes (M codes)
- Academic award recipients for research work: updated application instructions under the International Mobility Program, covering place of application, eligibility, documentary evidence, application assessment and final decisions, plus a new glossary term, "affiliated research institution," with its definition and applicable eligibility requirements
- All three updates are operational instructions for officers and do not directly change applicant eligibility or application streams
