# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (November 6, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

On November 6, 2026, Manitoba published a new provincial nomination selection round, Alberta updated its processing information and draw records, and the federal government announced that the Minister will make an announcement on skilled talent in rural and remote communities on November 9.

- Manitoba's Expression of Interest Draw #283 issued 526 Letters of Advice to Apply, including 448 in an occupation-specific selection under the Skilled Worker in Manitoba category and 78 under strategic recruitment initiatives.
- Alberta's processing information page was updated on November 4, showing 6,108 nominations issued in 2026, 495 spaces remaining, and five new draws issuing 425 invitations in total.
- The federal government issued a media advisory: the Minister will make an announcement on attracting and retaining skilled talent in rural and remote communities on November 9 in Stellarton, Nova Scotia.
- Federal Express Entry's latest round remained Round 453 of October 28, 2026, and no new round was published that day.

## Invitations and Selection Rounds

- Program: Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program, Expression of Interest Draw #283
- Publication date: November 5, 2026
- Total Letters of Advice to Apply issued: 526
- Skilled Worker in Manitoba, occupation-specific selection: 448, for candidates declaring current employment in Manitoba in natural resources, agriculture and related production occupations, or occupations in manufacturing and utilities
- Skilled Worker Stream, strategic recruitment selection: 78, broken down by invitation source as Employer Services 20, Ethnocultural Communities 11, Francophone Community 12, Regional Communities 30, and the Temporary Public Policy to Facilitate Work Permits for Prospective Provincial Nominee Program Candidates 5
- Of the 526, 49 were issued to candidates who declared a valid Express Entry profile number and job seeker validation code
- The previous round was Draw #282 on October 22, which issued 1,304 Letters of Advice to Apply
- Analysis: two weeks after a large selection of 1,304, Manitoba selected again at a smaller scale of 526, focused entirely on occupation-specific candidates already employed in Manitoba and candidates invited under strategic recruitment initiatives. No separate selection for Skilled Worker Overseas or the International Education Stream was published, and no minimum score was published, indicating a targeted top-up round rather than a broad reopening

- Program: Alberta Advantage Immigration Program processing information update
- Page update date: November 4, 2026
- 2026 nomination allocation: 6,603
- Nominations issued: 6,108, with 495 spaces remaining and 756 applications to be processed
- Expression of Interest pool total: 32,211
- New draw records:
- November 2, 2026, Rural Renewal Stream, minimum score 51, 173 invitations
- October 30, 2026, Alberta Opportunity Stream, minimum score 59, 132 invitations
- October 29, 2026, Dedicated Health Care Pathway, non-Express Entry, minimum score 50, 64 invitations
- October 27, 2026, Dedicated Health Care Pathway, Express Entry, minimum score 60, 19 invitations
- October 23, 2026, Rural Renewal Stream, minimum score 51, 37 invitations
- Total invitations across the five rounds: 425
- Additional federal spaces: 50 nominations issued for physicians and 12 for Francophones, which do not count toward the 6,603 allocation
- Analysis: Alberta held five draws in quick succession from late October to early November, issuing 425 invitations across the Rural Renewal, Opportunity, and Dedicated Health Care streams. With 495 nomination spaces left, about 7.5 percent of the allocation, and 32,211 expressions of interest still in the pool, competition remains intense. The Rural Renewal Stream's minimum score of 51 in both rounds was the lowest of the five
