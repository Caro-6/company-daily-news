# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (October 23, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

Today's official updates come from five provinces: Alberta refreshed its provincial nomination processing figures and published five new draws; Manitoba released the results of Expression of Interest Draw #282; British Columbia published the results of its October 20 Entrepreneur Immigration draw; Prince Edward Island updated the official figures for its October 16 draw; and Quebec's immigration ministry was officially renamed the Ministry of Immigration and Integration, with Catherine Gentilcore named minister. There were no new Express Entry rounds today.

## Invitations and Draws

- Alberta: provincial nomination processing information updated October 21. The 2026 allocation stands at 6,603 nominations, with 5,831 issued, 772 spaces remaining, and 856 applications to be processed; the province-wide expression-of-interest pool holds 33,291 profiles. Five new draws:
  - October 20, Express Entry Stream – Priority Sectors (General): minimum score 60, 386 invitations
  - October 16, Express Entry Stream – Law Enforcement Pathway: minimum score 49, fewer than 10 invitations
  - October 14, Tourism and Hospitality Stream: minimum score 73, 25 invitations
  - October 13, Dedicated Health Care Pathway – Express Entry: minimum score 57, 53 invitations
  - October 9, Express Entry Stream – Priority Sectors (Health care): minimum score 60, 97 invitations
- Manitoba: Expression of Interest Draw #282, held October 22, issued 1,304 Letters of Advice to Apply:
  - Skilled Worker in Manitoba – completed post-secondary study in Manitoba: 734 letters, lowest score 816
  - Overall top-scoring candidates: 513 letters, lowest score 846
  - Skilled Worker Stream (strategic recruitment): 57 letters — Employer Services 21, Ethnocultural Communities 2, Francophone Community 16, Regional Communities 15, temporary public policy 3
  - 353 letters went to candidates who declared a valid Express Entry profile
- British Columbia: the official invitations page carries Entrepreneur Immigration results. October 20 round: Base category, minimum score 118, 10 invitations; Regional category, minimum score 118, fewer than 5 invitations.
- Prince Edward Island: the official draw results now show 164 invitations for the October 16 round, bringing the 2026 total to 1,483.

## Announcements and Policy Changes

- Quebec: the provincial immigration ministry has been officially renamed the Ministry of Immigration and Integration (Ministère de l'Immigration et de l'Intégration, MII), with Catherine Gentilcore named minister, announced October 22. The ministry's pages now sit within the Quebec government website.
