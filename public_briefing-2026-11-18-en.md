## Announcements and Policy Changes

### Federal: Express Entry researchers category eligibility update

- Notice published: November 18, 2026; changes in effect: November 17, 2026
- Category: Express Entry researchers with Canadian work experience
- University professors and lecturers (NOC 41200): Canadian work experience and job offers paid by a Canadian entity through a stipend or other funding, such as grants, bursaries or scholarships, now count under Express Entry
- Such experience can now count toward the work experience requirements of the Canadian Experience Class and the Federal Skilled Worker Program, and qualifying candidates can also receive Comprehensive Ranking System points
- Candidates with qualifying Canadian experience should update their Express Entry profile to reflect it
- Post-secondary teaching and research assistants (NOC 41201): work experience no longer qualifies for the category, keeping it focused on university-level academic and research roles, including professors, lecturers and post-doctoral researchers

### Federal: Express Entry scoring update complete; draws not yet resumed

- The official changes page now lists two sets of changes in effect: researchers eligibility and category selection (November 17, 2026), and points for Canadian work experience and job offers (November 3, 2026)
- Points in effect since November 3, 2026: a new Labour Market Integration factor in the Comprehensive Ranking System, worth up to 150 points; points are awarded for either Canadian work experience or a qualifying job offer, not both
- Canadian work experience points combine work-length points with extra points for work in eligible high-wage occupations, up to 140 points with a spouse or common-law partner and 150 points without one
- Extra points for high-wage occupation experience: 70 points where the occupation's median wage is at least twice the average median wage, 40 points at 1.5 times or more, and 20 points at 1.3 times or more
- Job offer points: up to 100 points for a qualifying offer in an eligible high-wage occupation; a Labour Market Impact Assessment is no longer required for a job offer to qualify for program eligibility or points
- The official pages no longer carry a note pausing invitation rounds; the latest round remains Round 453 on October 28, 2026 (Transport Occupations, 150 invitations, lowest score 477), and the pool distribution remains the October 25, 2026 edition
