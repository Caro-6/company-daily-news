### IRCC: Open work permit instructions for spouses of study permit holders (C42) updated

On August 24, IRCC updated its program delivery instructions to clarify the application of the open work permit for spouses and common-law partners of study permit holders (R205(c)(ii) – C42) to spouses and partners of Francophone Minority Communities Student Pilot participants, with links to the pilot's operational instructions.
