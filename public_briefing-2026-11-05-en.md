# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (November 5, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

On November 5, 2026, the federal government released the 2027–2029 Immigration Levels Plan: permanent resident targets of 380,000 per year for three years, temporary resident arrival targets of 370,000 per year, higher targets for the Provincial Nominee Program and the Atlantic Immigration Program, rising French-speaking immigration targets outside Quebec, and a time-limited measure for 2027.

- The federal government released the 2027–2029 Immigration Levels Plan, with permanent resident targets of 380,000 per year and temporary resident arrival targets of 370,000 per year.
- The Provincial Nominee Program target is 95,000 per year, the Atlantic Immigration Program target is 6,000 per year, and the Rural Community Immigration Program target rises each year.
- French-speaking permanent resident targets outside Quebec are 9.5% in 2027, 10.5% in 2028, and 12% in 2029.
- Federal Express Entry's latest round remained Round 453 of October 28, 2026, the pause on rounds remained in place, and no new round was published that day.

## Announcements and Policy Changes

- Program: 2027–2029 Immigration Levels Plan
- Release date: November 5, 2026
- Permanent resident target: 380,000 per year in 2027, 2028, and 2029, within a range of 352,500 to 422,000
- Temporary resident arrival target: 370,000 per year in 2027, 2028, and 2029, within a range of 355,000 to 385,000
- Worker arrival target: 220,000 per year, including 170,000 under the International Mobility Program and 50,000 under the Temporary Foreign Worker Program
- Student arrival target: 150,000 per year
- Economic category share: 64.4% each year, with a total of 244,700 economic admissions per year
- Federal High Skilled: 100,000 per year
- Federal Business: 1,500 in 2027, 2,725 in 2028, and 1,000 in 2029
- Federal Economic Pilots: 7,900 in 2027, 6,175 in 2028, and 7,400 in 2029, covering home care workers, the Agri-Food Pilot, the Economic Mobility Pathways Pilot, the Francophone Community Immigration Pilot, and the Francophone Minority Communities Student Pilot
- Rural Community Immigration Program: 5,500 in 2027, 6,000 in 2028, and 6,500 in 2029
- Atlantic Immigration Program: 6,000 per year
- Provincial Nominee Program: 95,000 per year
- Quebec Skilled Workers and Business: target to be determined
- Family category total: 81,000 per year, including 66,000 spouses, partners, and children, and 15,000 parents and grandparents
- Refugees and Protected Persons total: 49,300 per year, including 20,000 protected persons in Canada and dependants abroad, 11,750 government-assisted refugees, and 17,550 privately sponsored refugees
- Humanitarian and Compassionate and Other total: 5,000 per year
- French-speaking permanent resident targets outside Quebec: 9.5% in 2027 (31,825 people), 10.5% in 2028 (35,175 people), and 12% in 2029 (40,200 people)
- Time-limited measure: to be implemented in 2027, with a workers cohort and a humanitarian cohort, for workers already in Canada and their accompanying family members, and for humanitarian applicants who have waited in Canada for many years; implementation details have not yet been published
- Continuing measure: the one-time initiatives announced in Budget 2025 will continue, accelerating up to 115,000 protected persons in Canada and their in-Canada dependants, and 33,000 work permit holders, to permanent residence in 2026 and 2027
- Compared with the previously released 2026–2028 Levels Plan: the overall permanent resident target and the temporary resident arrival target are unchanged; the Provincial Nominee Program rises from 92,500 to 95,000 per year, the Atlantic Immigration Program rises from 4,000 to 6,000 per year, the Federal High Skilled category falls from 111,000 to 100,000 per year, the Rural Community Immigration Program is listed as a separate line in the new plan, and the French-speaking target reaches 12% in 2029
- Analysis: The three-year targets are broadly stable, with permanent resident admissions held at 380,000 per year and temporary resident arrivals held at 370,000 per year. The structure continues to shift toward provincial and regional programs, with higher targets for the Provincial Nominee Program, the Atlantic Immigration Program, and the Rural Community Immigration Program. The Federal High Skilled target is lower, though a target figure is not the same as the number of invitations actually issued, which will depend on future rounds. The new time-limited measure for 2027 is aimed at applicants already in Canada, and its implementation details are still to be published.
