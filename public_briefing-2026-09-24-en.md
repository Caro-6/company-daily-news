## Latest Announcements

### IRCC (September 9 notice): Work permit holders may study without a study permit

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada announced on September 9 that work permit holders may study for up to 6 months (or until their work permit expires, whichever comes first) without a study permit, helping tradespeople, nurses, and lab technicians upskill while working. The measure is in effect until December 31, 2027; full-time study still requires a study permit. This is a temporary facilitation measure, not a new immigration pathway.
