## Latest Announcements

### IRCC: Permanent residence pathways for Hong Kong residents close on August 31

On August 31, the permanent residence pathways for Hong Kong residents (Stream A for in-Canada graduates and Stream B for Canadian work experience) officially closed; no new applications are being accepted. Applications submitted on or before August 31, 2026 will continue to be processed. The accompanying open work permit policy remains available through May 2029.
