- Three confirmed invitation updates from official channels today: Newfoundland and Labrador issued 62 invitations on September 18; Alberta's September 18 Priority Sectors (Health Care) targeted selection issued 100 invitations; New Brunswick's Skilled Worker stream issued 197 invitations over September 15–18.

## Invitation Rounds

### Newfoundland and Labrador: 62 invitations issued on September 18

On September 18, Newfoundland and Labrador issued 62 invitations to apply — 61 through the Provincial Nominee Program (NLPNP) and 1 through the Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP).

### Alberta: September 18 Priority Sectors (Health Care) targeted selection

On September 18, Alberta's Express Entry Priority Sectors (Health Care) stream issued 100 invitations, with a minimum EOI score of 62.

### New Brunswick: Skilled Worker stream issues 197 invitations (September 15–18)

Over September 15–18, New Brunswick's Skilled Worker stream issued 197 invitations across two pathways: New Brunswick Graduates and New Brunswick Experience; the round cut-off was 11:59 p.m. Atlantic Time on September 13. Occupational categories covered: Construction; Education, social and community services; Manufacturing; Other trades; Professional and IT; Sales and services.
