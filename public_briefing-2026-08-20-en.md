## Invitation Rounds

### BC PNP: August 20 "Innovate: High Economic Impact" round

On August 20, BC PNP's "Innovate: High Economic Impact" round issued 602 invitations in total: 337 to candidates with a job offer in NOC TEER 0/1/2/3 occupations paying at least $55 per hour and $110,000 per year (no points cut-off), and 265 to candidates with a minimum registration score of 132.

### Prince Edward Island: 181 invitations issued on August 20

On August 20, Prince Edward Island issued 181 invitations to apply, all in the Labour Impact and PEI Express Entry categories; the Business Work Permit Entrepreneur category issued no invitations in this round. Following this draw, PEI has issued 1,034 invitations in 2026.

### New Brunswick: Atlantic Immigration Program selects 68 applications across August batches

Across New Brunswick's Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP) selection batches of August 10 and 20, 68 applications were selected for processing in total; the batch cut-offs were 11:59 p.m. Atlantic Time on August 9 and August 19 respectively. Occupational categories covered: Construction trades; Education, social and community services; Manufacturing; Other trades; Professional and IT; Sales and services; Transportation.
