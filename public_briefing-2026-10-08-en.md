# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (October 8, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

Three groups of official updates today. Manitoba held Expression of Interest Draw #281 (October 8), issuing 340 Letters of Advice to Apply with a focus on health and education occupations; Alberta's AAIP processing information page was updated with four new draw records, and 2026 nomination issuance has advanced to 84% of the allocation; and the BC PNP Skills Immigration registration pool snapshot was refreshed to October 6, with 7,695 registrations in the pool. IRCC's federal channels (news releases, notices, Express Entry rounds) had no new items today, and all other provincial channels show no changes.

## Invitations and draw results

- Manitoba Draw #281 (October 8)
  - This draw issued 340 Letters of Advice to Apply (LAAs) in total.
  - Occupation-specific selections: 46 LAAs for health occupations (Broad Occupational Category 3); 211 LAAs for education occupations, covering NOC 41220 (secondary school teachers), NOC 41221 (elementary school and kindergarten teachers), NOC 42202 (early childhood educators and assistants), and NOC 43100 (elementary and secondary school teacher assistants).
  - Strategic recruitment initiatives: 83 LAAs, including Employer Services 45, Ethnocultural Communities 9, Francophone Community 16, Regional Communities 12, and the Temporary Public Policy work-permit pathway 1.
  - Of the 340 LAAs, 103 went to candidates who declared a valid Express Entry profile number and job seeker validation code.
- Four new Alberta AAIP draws
  - October 7: Alberta Express Entry Stream – Priority Sectors (Aviation), minimum score 56, 22 invitations.
  - October 2: Alberta Opportunity Stream, minimum score 54, 123 invitations.
  - September 29: Alberta Express Entry Stream – Priority Sectors (General), minimum score 64, 200 invitations.
  - September 24: Rural Renewal Stream, minimum score 51, 131 invitations.
  - All four were small, targeted sector or stream draws, with minimum scores between 51 and 64.

## Announcements and policy changes

- Alberta AAIP processing information updated (October 7)
  - The official processing information page was updated to October 7, 2026, refreshing nomination and expression-of-interest pool figures.
  - For 2026: nomination allocation 6,603, nominations issued 5,541 (84% of allocation), nomination spaces remaining 1,062, applications to be processed 948.
  - The expression-of-interest pool holds 34,478 registrations: Alberta Opportunity Stream 21,667; Priority sector draws and other initiatives 4,912; Tourism and Hospitality 3,138; Accelerated Tech Pathway 1,964; Rural Renewal Stream 1,570; Dedicated Health Care Pathways 1,179; Law Enforcement Pathway 48.
- BC PNP registration pool snapshot refreshed
  - The Skills Immigration registration pool snapshot was updated to October 6, with 7,695 registrations in the pool, down 318 from the September 1 snapshot.
  - Score-band distribution: 150+ 9; 140–149 23; 130–139 171; 120–129 854; 110–119 1,189; 100–109 1,426; 90–99 1,444; 80–89 1,281; 70–79 718; 60–69 364; 0–59 216.
  - No new invitation rounds; the latest remain the two September 24 Innovate rounds (426 + 288 invitations, minimum score 131).
