# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (2026-10-27)

## Today's Highlights

On October 27, Express Entry held a new French-language category round, issuing 5,000 invitations with a minimum CRS score of 407. On the same day, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada issued a new notice: the temporary measures for Sudanese and Haitian nationals in Canada who cannot return home at this time, originally set to expire on October 27, are extended to December 30, 2027. Apart from these items, provincial official channels show no changes.

## Latest Announcements and Policy Changes

- Federal: temporary measures for Sudanese and Haitian nationals extended to December 30, 2027
  - Who is affected: Sudanese and Haitian nationals in Canada who cannot return home at this time
  - Previous expiry date: October 27, 2026
  - New expiry date: December 30, 2027
  - Work permits: eligible people who hold a valid work permit issued under the previous temporary measures can apply to extend their permit, with standard processing fees
