## Latest Announcements and Policy Changes
- IRCC extends temporary Ebola-related immigration measures
- Announcement date: September 28, 2026
- Measures extended by 60 days, until November 27, 2026
- Immigration documents remain suspended for residents of the Democratic Republic of Congo, Uganda and South Sudan; even those holding previously approved visas, permits or electronic travel authorizations cannot travel to Canada at this time
- IRCC will continue processing affected new and existing applications, but no final decisions will be made while the measures are in place
