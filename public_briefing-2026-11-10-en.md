# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (November 10, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

On November 10, 2026, the federal government announced faster processing of permanent residence applications from Ukrainians and Hong Kongers already in Canada, a Start-up Business Class assessment update, 57 new invitations from Newfoundland and Labrador, and the official program page confirmed the opening date of the permanent Rural Community Immigration Program.

- The federal government announced faster processing of permanent residence applications from Ukrainians and Hong Kongers already in Canada, using 2027 admission spaces to process all remaining applications under the Hong Kong pathways, up to 32,000 admissions, and the Ukraine Family Reunification Pathway, up to 18,800 admissions.
- A federal Start-up Business Class program delivery update clarifies that only applications where at least one member of the group holds a valid A77 work permit qualify for prioritization, with no change to eligibility or fees.
- Newfoundland and Labrador issued 57 invitations on November 9: 35 under the Provincial Nominee Program and 22 under the Atlantic Immigration Program.
- The Rural Community Immigration Program's official page confirms the permanent program opens January 1, 2027, with fees from $1,590, and the current pilot closes the same day.
- Federal Express Entry's latest round remained Round 453 of October 28, 2026, and no new round was published that day.

## Guide Updates

- Program: Federal Start-up Business Class application assessment program delivery instructions update
- Update date: November 10, 2026
- Summary: the Start-up Business Class assessment instructions were updated to clarify that, for the purposes of prioritization, only a work permit with the code A77 will be considered. A work permit with the code A77 is available only to Start-up Business Class applicants. Applications where at least one member of the application group holds a valid A77 work permit are prioritized first; applications where no member holds a valid A77 work permit but the application meets one or more of the other prioritization conditions come next; all other applications are processed in the order they are received
- Analysis: this is a clarification that pins down which work permit code counts for prioritization. It affects only the order in which applications are accepted into processing and changes no eligibility criteria, settlement funds, language requirements, or fees

## Announcements and Policy Changes

- Program: Federal faster processing of permanent residence applications from Ukrainians and Hong Kongers
- Announcement date: November 10, 2026
- Announcement location: Ottawa
- Summary: a time-limited measure under the 2027–2029 Immigration Levels Plan creates a humanitarian cohort to accelerate processing of permanent residence applications from Ukrainians and Hong Kongers waiting in Canada. Admission spaces in 2027 will be used to process all remaining applications under the Hong Kong permanent residence pathways, which closed to new applications in August 2026 and are expected to result in up to 32,000 admissions, and all remaining applications under the Ukraine Family Reunification Pathway, which closed to new applications in October 2024 and is expected to result in up to 18,800 admissions. The applicants are already living and working in Canada and do not represent net new arrivals. The measure sits outside the Humanitarian and Compassionate and Other category targets in the levels plan and does not displace other vulnerable populations, including resettled refugees. The Minister of Immigration met with leaders of Canada's Ukrainian and Hong Kong communities on November 7
- Analysis: this is the first concrete follow-through on clearing backlogs since the levels plan was published, aiming to process all remaining applications under two closed pathways within 2027. Because the applicants are already in Canada, the measure adds no new arrivals and focuses on cutting wait times and ending uncertainty over status. Applicants with pending Hong Kong pathway or Ukraine Family Reunification Pathway applications who are waiting in Canada can expect faster processing in 2027

- Program: Rural Community Immigration Program permanent stream opening date and pilot closing date
- Summary: the official program page confirms the permanent program opens January 1, 2027, with fees from $1,590, and the current Rural Community Immigration Pilot closes the same day. Applicants holding a community recommendation dated December 31, 2026, or earlier must apply for permanent residence before it expires. Applicants without a pilot recommendation can consider the new permanent program. Applications already submitted will continue to be processed, and eligible applicants can still apply for a work permit so they can work while their application is processed
- Analysis: the opening date of the permanent program and the closing date of the pilot are now set out on the official program page, so applicants and communities can plan recommendations and applications around them. Applicants holding a recommendation from 2026 should watch its expiry date so it does not lapse
