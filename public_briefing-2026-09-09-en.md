## Invitation Rounds

### Alberta: September 9 Dedicated Health Care Pathway (Express Entry) selection

On September 9, Alberta's Express Entry Dedicated Health Care Pathway issued 51 invitations, with a minimum EOI score of 60.

## Latest Announcements

### IRCC: Work permit holders in Canada may study without a study permit

On September 9, IRCC announced a temporary, time-limited measure: eligible work permit holders in Canada may study for up to 6 months (or until their work permit expires, whichever comes first) without a study permit; IRCC will notify eligible holders directly; full-time study still requires a study permit. The measure is in effect until December 31, 2027 and is not a new immigration pathway.
