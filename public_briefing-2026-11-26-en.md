## Program Delivery Updates

- November 26, 2026, open work permit renewals linked to the crises in Sudan and Haiti: operational guidance for renewing open work permits for foreign nationals in Canada who were facilitated under previous public policies related to the crises in Sudan and Haiti; the temporary public policies took effect on October 28, 2026 and expire on December 30, 2027
- November 26, 2026, expiry of the public policy on previously undeclared family members: the public policy exempting certain previously undeclared family members from exclusion under the relevant provisions expired on September 10, 2026; applications received before expiry continue to be processed under the policy, while those received after expiry do not; the update also adds more detailed guidance on assessing the exclusions and applying humanitarian and compassionate considerations
