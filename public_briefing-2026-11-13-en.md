## Invitation Rounds

### British Columbia: November 13 Care and Build invitations

- Round date: November 13, 2026
- Category: BC Provincial Nominee Program, Skills Immigration, for priority occupations in Care and Build
- Care – Childcare: early childhood educators only, minimum score 95, 153 invitations
- Care – Health: all priority health care occupations, minimum score 72, 103 invitations
- Care – Veterinary Care: all priority veterinary care occupations, including animal health technologists and veterinary technicians (NOC 32104) with a valid professional designation, minimum score 100, fewer than 5 invitations
- Build – Construction Trades: all priority construction occupations, minimum score 94, 128 invitations
- Total this round: more than 384 invitations (the Veterinary Care count is published as fewer than 5, with no exact figure)
- Compared with the previous Care and Build round on October 8: Childcare 86 invitations at a minimum score of 99, Health 82 at 76, Veterinary Care fewer than 5 at 86, Construction 115 at 94; invitation numbers rose in all four categories this round, and the minimum scores for Childcare and Health both fell
- Registration pool: still 7,235 as of November 3, not updated with this round
- Entrepreneur Immigration: latest round remains October 20, with 10 invitations in the Base category at a minimum score of 118 and fewer than 5 in the Regional category at 118
