# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing · October 16, 2026 (Evening Update)

## Today's Highlights

- One new item tonight: Express Entry held its 450th round of invitations, a French-language proficiency round issuing 5,000 invitations with a minimum CRS score of 435.
- The round took place this morning; the official rounds page had not yet been updated when this afternoon's special edition was published, so it is reported here as an evening update. No other official channels published new changes tonight.
- After the high-wage occupation points changes take effect on November 3, invitation rounds will pause for up to two weeks while scores are recalculated.

## Express Entry Rounds of Invitations

- Round: 450th round, French-Language proficiency (2026-Version 2), category-based round
- Date and time of round: October 16, 2026 at 10:25:13 UTC
- Number of invitations issued: 5,000
- CRS score of lowest-ranked candidate invited: 435
- Tie-breaking rule: candidates with the cut-off score who submitted their profiles before October 6, 2026 at 21:31:22 UTC were ranked ahead
- Who was eligible: candidates with French-language test results at NCLC 7 in all four abilities who meet the requirements of the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Canadian Experience Class, or the Federal Skilled Trades Program
- Context: the previous French-language round was held on August 19 (cut-off 382); after a gap of nearly two months, the cut-off has risen to 435. This is one of the last rounds before the new points system takes effect on November 3
