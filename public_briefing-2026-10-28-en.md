## Latest Announcements and Policy Changes

- Federal: new penalties for immigration and citizenship representatives who break the law take effect today
  - Effective date: October 28, 2026
  - Who is affected: people who break the law while providing paid immigration or citizenship advice or representation, including licensed immigration consultants, lawyers and other authorized representatives, as well as unauthorized paid providers
  - Penalties: $5,000 for providing paid advice or representation without authorization; $15,000 for knowingly providing false or misleading information on an applicant's behalf, or helping an applicant do so
  - Additional and repeat penalties: additional penalties may apply if an application is approved based on false or misleading information; penalties increase for repeat violations, up to a combined maximum of $1.5 million
  - Publication: as penalties are issued, the department will publish the names of those penalized and the details of their violations on its website; an online registry is expected to launch in 2027, following the administration of the first penalties
  - Advice for applicants: check that a representative is authorized before paying for services
  - This is an enforcement and integrity measure, not a new immigration pathway, and it does not change application eligibility
