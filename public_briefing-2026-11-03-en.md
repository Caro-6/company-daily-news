# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (2026-11-03)

## Today's Highlights

- Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada published a news release on November 3, 2026, modernizing the asylum system, with new regulations under the Strengthening Canada's Immigration System and Borders Act coming into force the same day and moving asylum claims to a single online process.
- The Express Entry reform announced on October 16, 2026, took effect on November 3, 2026, and the department is recalculating all profiles in the pool under the new scoring, a process taking up to about two weeks, during which invitation rounds are paused.
- There was no new Express Entry round today, and the latest round remains Round 453 of October 28, 2026; no other province published a new draw or policy change today.

## Latest Announcements and Policy Changes

- Federal government modernizes the asylum system
- Publication date: November 3, 2026
- Category: News release
- Minister: Lena Metlege Diab
- Effective date: November 3, 2026
- Legal basis: New regulations under the Strengthening Canada's Immigration System and Borders Act
- Main changes: A single online application process for all asylum claims, whether made inland or at a port of entry; clearer requirements and timelines; timelines for key government reviews; rules for reinstatement of withdrawn claims and claims that are not abandoned; stronger support for vulnerable claimants; access to work permits for individuals with eligible claims; exceptions to the new ineligibility rules
- Claim volumes: Asylum claims made in Canada from January to August 2026 were down 52% from the same period in 2025 and 68% from the same period in 2024
- What this means: This is a procedural reform of the asylum process, not a new economic immigration program, quota, or scoring change, and it has no direct effect on economic applicants; asylum claimants will now use a single online channel, with clearer process steps and timelines, while detailed operational guidance remains to be published by the department

- Express Entry reform takes effect, with invitation rounds paused while scores are recalculated
- Effective date: November 3, 2026
- Recalculation scope: All profiles in the pool
- Recalculation time: Up to about two weeks
- Round status: Invitation rounds are paused during recalculation and will resume once it is finished, with the score distribution table updated at that time
- Scoring tool update: The Express Entry score tool has been updated to November 3, 2026, with new questions on work experience in high-wage occupations, including whether the candidate has at least 1 year or 1,560 hours of skilled Canadian work experience in an occupation on the high-wage occupation list within the last 10 years
- Latest round: Still Round 453 of October 28, 2026, Transport Occupations, 150 invitations, lowest score 477
- What this means: Today marks the reform reaching its effective date and entering system implementation, not a further change to the rules; there is no new round to join in the short term, and the score distribution table will not be updated until recalculation is complete; candidates in the pool should make sure the occupation, work experience, and job offer information in their profiles is accurate, since recalculated scores depend on the information already in the profile
