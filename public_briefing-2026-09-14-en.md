## Invitation Rounds

### Saskatchewan: Capped-sector intake windows open on September 14

On September 14, the Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program opened three capped-sector intake windows (Employer Position Assessment intakes): Trucking (quota 150, 102 used, 48 remaining), Retail (quota 175, 129 used, 46 remaining), and Accommodations (quota 50, 50 used, 0 remaining — filled the same day). These are employer position quotas, not invitation rounds.
