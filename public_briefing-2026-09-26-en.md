## Invitation Rounds

### Newfoundland and Labrador: 41 invitations issued on September 25

Newfoundland and Labrador issued 41 invitations to apply (ITAs) on September 25. All 41 invitations were issued through the Newfoundland and Labrador Provincial Nominee Program (NLPNP); the Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP) issued no invitations in this round.

Compared with the previous round on September 18, total invitations decreased by 21. The previous round issued 62 invitations — 61 through the NLPNP and 1 through the AIP.

## Latest Announcements

### IRCC: News conference on Francophone immigration initiatives scheduled for September 29

On September 25, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) issued a media advisory announcing that the Honourable Lena Metlege Diab, Minister of Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship, will hold a news conference in St. John's on Tuesday, September 29 at 10:00 a.m. (Newfoundland Time, 9:30 a.m. Atlantic Time) to announce innovative projects aimed at strengthening Francophone immigration in Newfoundland and Labrador and in Francophone minority communities outside Quebec.

The Honourable Lin Paddock, Newfoundland and Labrador Minister of Jobs, Growth and Rural Development, will join the Minister, and the news conference will be livestreamed on YouTube. This is a media advisory only; policy details will be confirmed when the announcement is made on September 29.
