## Invitation Rounds

### New Brunswick: Strategic Initiative issues 251 invitations on September 11

On September 11, New Brunswick's Strategic Initiative issued 251 invitations across two pathways: Francophone Workers in New Brunswick and New Brunswick Francophone Priorities; the round cut-off was 11:59 p.m. Atlantic Time on September 8. Occupational categories covered: Construction; Education, social and community services; Manufacturing; Other trades; Professional and IT; Sales and services; Transportation.
