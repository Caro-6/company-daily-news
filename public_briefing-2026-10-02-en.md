## Today's Highlights

- The federal government updated the pre-removal risk assessment (PRRA) 12-month wait-period exemption list, adding Israel and Palestine; people who received a negative decision between September 28, 2025 and September 29, 2026 may re-apply for a PRRA before 12 months have passed (official page dated September 29, 2026).

## Latest Announcements and Policy Changes

- Pre-removal risk assessment (PRRA) 12-month wait-period exemption list updated (September 29, 2026, IRCC)
  - Israel and Palestine added to the exemption list
  - People from these countries who received a negative decision on or between September 28, 2025 and September 29, 2026 may apply for a PRRA before 12 months have passed
  - IRCC notes the exemption applies when conditions in the country of origin change suddenly; applicants must come from an exempt country and their negative decision must fall within the listed date range
