# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (November 17, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

- New Brunswick published three rounds of invitations and selections: the Atlantic Immigration Program selected 73 applications on November 6 and 16, the Skilled Worker stream issued 198 invitations on November 13, and the Strategic Initiative stream issued 192 invitations on November 2, for a combined total of 463.
- British Columbia held a new Entrepreneur Immigration draw on November 17, with a minimum score of 117 for both the Base and Regional categories: 12 invitations in the Base category and fewer than 5 in the Regional category.
- There were no new releases from the federal newsroom today, and Express Entry draws remain paused, with the latest round still Round 453 on October 28. Newfoundland and Labrador, Prince Edward Island, Alberta, Manitoba, Ontario, Saskatchewan, Nova Scotia and Quebec reported no new draws or announcements today, and New Brunswick's five program guides have no new versions.

## Invitations and Selections

### New Brunswick: 463 invitations and selections across three rounds

- Atlantic Immigration Program
  - Draw dates: November 6 and 16, 2026
  - Cut-off: November 5 and 15, 2026, 11:59 pm (Atlantic Standard Time)
  - Applications selected: 73
  - Occupational categories: Construction; Education, social and community services; Health care; Manufacturing; Other trades; Professional and IT; Sales and services; Transportation
- Skilled Worker stream
  - Draw date: November 13, 2026
  - Cut-off: November 10, 2026, 11:59 pm (Atlantic Standard Time)
  - Invitations issued: 198
  - Pathways: New Brunswick Graduates; New Brunswick Experience
  - Occupational categories: Construction; Education, social and community services; Health care; Manufacturing; Other trades; Professional and IT; Sales and services
  - The previous round was September 15 to 18 (197 invitations), making this the first round in nearly two months
- Strategic Initiative stream
  - Draw date: November 2, 2026
  - Cut-off: November 1, 2026, 11:59 pm (Atlantic Standard Time)
  - Invitations issued: 192
  - Pathways: Francophone Workers in New Brunswick; New Brunswick Francophone Priorities
  - Occupational categories: Construction; Education, social and community services; Manufacturing; Other trades; Professional and IT; Sales and services; Transportation
  - The previous round was September 11 (251 invitations), making this the first round in nearly two months
- What this means: the Skilled Worker round covered both the Graduates and Experience pathways, and the province did not publish a breakdown by pathway. The restriction limiting New Brunswick Experience invitations to health care, education and construction, in place since May 4, remains in effect. An invitation is not a nomination or a permanent residence approval, and invited candidates must submit a complete application within the stated deadline. The Atlantic Immigration Program figure counts applications selected for processing.

### British Columbia: Entrepreneur Immigration draw on November 17, minimum score down to 117

- Draw date: November 17, 2026
- Base category: minimum score 117, 12 invitations
- Regional category: minimum score 117, fewer than 5 invitations
- Previous draw (October 20, 2026): Base category minimum 118, 10 invitations; Regional category minimum 118, fewer than 5 invitations
- What this means: the minimum score fell by 1 point in both categories, and Base invitations rose from 10 to 12. Entrepreneur draws run about every four weeks, and the threshold has eased slightly while volumes remain small. Skills Immigration last drew on November 13 for care and construction occupations, with no new round today.
