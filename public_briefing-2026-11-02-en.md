## Latest Announcements and Policy Changes

- Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada: statement marking the 14th National Francophone Immigration Week
- Date issued: November 2, 2026
- Category: statement
- Minister: Lena Metlege Diab
- Event: 14th National Francophone Immigration Week
- Theme: Finding your place within the Francophonie community
- Main message: the statement highlights the important contribution of Francophone immigrants to the vitality and influence of Francophone communities, noting that their talent, energy and dedication help Francophone and Acadian minority communities outside Quebec thrive
- This is a commemorative statement, not a new immigration program, quota or policy change, and it does not change the application requirements, scores or deadlines of any program
