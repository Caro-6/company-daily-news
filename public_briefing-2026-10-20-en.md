# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (2026-10-20)

## Today's Highlights

Two confirmed updates from official channels today: the federal International Experience Canada (IEC) 2026 season is officially closed and its draws have ended; Prince Edward Island's Provincial Nominee Program (PEI PNP) released three previously undisclosed rounds of selection results, bringing 2026 invitations to 1,482. No new rounds, guide updates, or policy changes were published by other federal or provincial channels today.

## Announcements and Policy Changes

- The IEC 2026 season is officially closed: federal officials confirm the 2026 candidate pools are closed, with the week of October 19 marking the final round of the 2026 season
- Applicants who received an invitation to apply may still complete and submit their work permit application
- Applicants who did not receive an invitation will have their profiles removed from the pools; they may create a new profile when the pools reopen for the 2027 season, while existing accounts and GCKeys remain valid with no need to re-register
