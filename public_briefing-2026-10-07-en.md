# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (October 7, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

Three official updates today. IRCC issued a notice inviting Ukrainian temporary residents in Canada to take part in a client experience survey; Ontario's OINP updated the "Canadian work experience: earnings history" scoring factor in the Ontario Workforce Priority stream's EOI system — registrants with single-year earnings of $80,000 or more within the past five years can now earn 10 points; and Saskatchewan refreshed its 2026 nomination allocation and issued-nomination figures, with the allocation rising to 5,261 and 4,023 nominations issued. The latest Express Entry round remains Round 447 from October 1; all other federal and provincial channels show no new activity.

## Announcements and policy changes

- IRCC client experience survey notice (October 7)
  - IRCC published a new notice, "Client experience survey for Ukrainian temporary residents in Canada," inviting Ukrainian temporary residents in Canada to participate in a client experience survey.
  - Randomly selected clients, including former CUAET applicants, will receive an email invitation; the survey runs in October, takes about 15–20 minutes, and is offered in English, French, Ukrainian, and Russian.
  - Participation is voluntary and confidential, and does not affect any past, current, or future application; results will be used to understand trends and client expectations.
  - Invitations come from noreply@cic.gc.ca, with survey links beginning https://ircc.qualtrics.com/jfe/form. IRCC reminds everyone to stay alert to phone and online scams impersonating the survey.
- OINP scoring update for the Ontario Workforce Priority stream (October 7 update)
  - On October 5, the OINP updated the "Canadian work experience: earnings history" scoring factor in the Ontario Workforce Priority stream's Expression of Interest system.
  - Registrants who earned $80,000 or more in a single year within the past five years, as verified by a Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) Notice of Assessment, now receive 10 points under this factor.
  - The change has been applied automatically to all existing EOIs; registrants do not need to make any changes or log into the e-Filing Portal.
- Saskatchewan updates 2026 nomination figures
  - The SINP refreshed its "Nominations Issued in 2026" table (figures updated quarterly).
  - The 2026 total allocation is 5,261, with 4,023 nominations issued to date, representing 76% of the allocation; priority sectors and other sectors stand at 3,946 allocated and 2,865 issued (73%), while trucking, accommodations, food services, and retail trade (capped at 25%) stand at 1,315 allocated and 1,158 issued (88%).
  - Compared with the previously published 4,761 allocation and 2,628 issued (55%), the allocation has grown by 500 and issuance has moved markedly faster.
