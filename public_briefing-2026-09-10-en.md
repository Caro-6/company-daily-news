# Canada Immigration Briefing (September 10, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

- Three confirmed invitation updates from official channels today: Alberta's September 10 Priority Sectors (Manufacturing) targeted selection issued 48 invitations; Newfoundland and Labrador issued 36 invitations on September 10, all through the Provincial Nominee Program; New Brunswick's Atlantic Immigration Program selected 60 applications for processing in this round.
- All other official channels are unchanged today.

## Invitation Rounds

### Alberta: September 10 Priority Sectors (Manufacturing) targeted selection

On September 10, Alberta's Express Entry Priority Sectors (Manufacturing) stream issued 48 invitations, with a minimum EOI score of 60.

### Newfoundland and Labrador: 36 invitations issued on September 10

On September 10, Newfoundland and Labrador issued 36 invitations to apply, all through the Provincial Nominee Program (NLPNP); the Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP) issued no invitations in this round.

### New Brunswick: Atlantic Immigration Program selects 60 applications

In New Brunswick's Atlantic Immigration Program selection round of September 10, 60 applications were selected for processing; the round cut-off was 11:59 p.m. Atlantic Time on September 9. Occupational categories covered: Construction; Education, social and community services; Manufacturing; Other trades; Professional and IT; Sales and services; Transportation.
