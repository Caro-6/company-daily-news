## Latest Announcements and Policy Changes

- Federal International Experience Canada (IEC): the pools for the 2026 season are now closed
  - Candidates who already received an invitation can still complete and submit their work permit application
  - Candidates who did not receive an invitation will have their profile removed from the pool and can create a new profile when the pools reopen for the 2027 season; their existing account remains valid and there is no need to register again
