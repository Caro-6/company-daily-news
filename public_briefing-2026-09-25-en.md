# Canada Immigration Briefing (September 25, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

- Three confirmed changes from official channels today: **BC PNP's September 24 Innovate: High Economic Impact rounds issued 714 invitations in total**; **Alberta added six small targeted draws between September 10 and 23, with 2026 nominations issued rising to 5,221**; **Manitoba's Draw #280 (September 24) issued 474 Letters of Advice**.
- All other official channels are unchanged today.

## Invitation Rounds

### BC PNP: September 24 Innovate rounds — 714 invitations

On September 24, BC PNP ran two rounds under the Innovate: High Economic Impact category: the wage-criteria row (minimum $52/hour and $105,000/year, NOC TEER 0/1/2/3 occupations) issued 426 invitations (no score requirement), and the 131-point row issued 288 invitations — 714 in total. This is the fourth Innovate round of 2026 (previous rounds: June 18, July 16, August 20).

The category shows a clear trend of falling thresholds and growing round sizes: June 18 ($62/hour, $125k/year, 136 points, 279 invitations) → July 16 ($58/$115k, 132 points, 569) → August 20 ($55/$110k, 132 points, 602) → September 24 ($52/$105k, 131 points, 714). This is increasingly favourable for candidates who meet the wage criteria.

### Alberta: Six small targeted draws added (September 10–23)

Alberta's processing information page is now updated to September 23, adding six draws: September 23 Alberta Opportunity Stream (58 points, 113 invitations), September 22 Accelerated Tech Pathway (59 points, 100 invitations), September 21 Law Enforcement Pathway (49 points, fewer than 10 invitations), September 18 Priority Sectors – Health Care (62 points, 100 invitations), September 15 Priority Sectors – Agriculture (60 points, 18 invitations), and September 10 Priority Sectors – Manufacturing (60 points, 48 invitations).

Of the 2026 allocation of 6,603 nominations, 5,221 have been issued (up 357 from the previous baseline) and 1,382 remain — roughly 79% used. Applications awaiting processing stand at 1,092, and the EOI pool at 35,596. All six rounds are small and targeted (the largest issued 113 invitations), and remaining spaces continue to skew toward health care, technology, and priority sectors.

### Manitoba: Draw #280 (September 24) — 474 invitations

Draw #280 on September 24 issued 474 Letters of Advice to Apply: 1 occupation-specific (NOC 31102, general practitioners), 417 in the Natural and applied sciences broad occupational category (minimum score 760), 16 francophone, and 40 strategic recruitment (Employer Services 9, Ethnocultural 9, Francophone Community 6, Regional 15, TPP 1); 148 went to candidates with valid Express Entry profiles.

This draw is smaller than #279 (793 invitations). The natural/applied sciences category dominates this round (417 invitations), while the Manitoba graduates/spouses category that led #279 (508 invitations, minimum 825) is absent this time — targeted selections are clearly rotating between occupational focuses.
