## Latest Announcements

### IRCC: Concurrent-processing grace period for LMIA-less TFWP extensions extended to 90 days

On August 21, IRCC updated its program delivery instructions: for temporary foreign workers in Canada whose permits are expiring and who have filed a complete LMIA application that has not yet been decided, the concurrent-processing grace period for work permit extension applications filed without an LMIA is extended from 60 to 90 days; a new tracking code, CPTS2026, is added to the intended-occupation field; all other eligibility criteria are unchanged.
