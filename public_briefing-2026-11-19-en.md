# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (November 19, 2026)

## Today's Highlights

- Newfoundland and Labrador issued a new batch of 98 invitations on November 19, including 32 under the Provincial Nominee Program and 66 under the Atlantic Immigration Program, bringing the 2026 total to 3,479.
- Manitoba held Draw #284 on November 19, issuing 1,116 Letters of Advice to Apply, including 1,050 under the Citizens of Ukraine selection and 66 under strategic recruitment invitations.
- Alberta updated its processing information page, adding draws on November 6, 9 and 13, with 2026 nominations issued rising to 6,440 and 163 spaces remaining.
- Nova Scotia reported 1,296 selections in October, the highest monthly figure this year, bringing the January–October total to 6,696.
- A new federal program delivery update revises the instructions for open work permits for Start-up Business Class permanent residence applicants.

## Invitations and Draws

### Newfoundland and Labrador: New batch of 98 invitations on November 19, 2026 total reaches 3,479

- Date issued: November 19, 2026
- Invitations issued: 98, including 32 under the NLPNP and 66 under the AIP
- 2026 total: 3,479 invitations across 26 rounds
- The previous batch was 54 invitations on November 16, including 12 under the NLPNP and 42 under the AIP
- This is the sixth batch in November and the largest single batch this month, with 66 of the 98 invitations under the AIP

### Manitoba: Draw #284 (November 19, 2026), 1,116 Letters of Advice to Apply issued

- Draw date: November 19, 2026
- Total Letters of Advice to Apply issued: 1,116
- Citizens of Ukraine selection: 1,050, for profiles whose principal applicant is a citizen of Ukraine and who meet Skilled Worker in Manitoba pathway eligibility
- Strategic recruitment invitations: 66, including Employer Services 32, Ethnocultural Communities 8, Francophone Community 16, Regional Communities 7, and the Temporary Public Policy to Facilitate Work Permits for Prospective Provincial Nominee Program Candidates 3
- Of the 1,116 letters, 41 went to candidates who declared a valid Express Entry profile number and job seeker validation code
- This draw used category-based selection and no minimum score was published
- The previous draw was Draw #283 on November 5, with 526 letters issued

### Alberta: Processing information updated, three new draws, nominations issued rise to 6,440

- Page last updated: November 18, 2026
- 2026 nomination allocation: 6,603; nominations issued rose from 6,108 to 6,440; spaces remaining fell from 495 to 163; applications to be processed fell from 756 to 717
- The Expression of Interest pool fell from 32,211 to 31,700, including Alberta Opportunity Stream 20,300, Dedicated Health Care Pathways 1,008, Rural Renewal Stream 1,163, Tourism and Hospitality Stream 2,718, and Alberta Express Entry Stream 6,511 (Accelerated Tech Pathway 1,854, Law Enforcement Pathway 38, priority sector draws and other initiatives 4,619)
- Three new draws added:
  - November 13, Alberta Opportunity Stream, minimum score 55, 241 invitations
  - November 9, Tourism and Hospitality Stream, minimum score 72, 20 invitations
  - November 6, Rural Renewal Stream, minimum score 51, 113 invitations
- The previous latest draw was November 2, Rural Renewal Stream, minimum score 51, 173 invitations
- About 97.5 percent of the 2026 allocation has now been used, leaving limited room for the rest of the year

### Nova Scotia: 1,296 selections in October, January–October total reaches 6,696

- October 2026 selections: 1,296, the highest monthly figure in 2026
- January–September 2026 total: 5,400; January–October total: 6,696
- Figures for November and December 2026 have not yet been published
- Selections continue to focus on healthcare occupations, skilled trades and other priority occupations aligned with labour market needs

## Announcements and Policy Changes

### Federal: Instructions updated for open work permits for Start-up Business Class applicants

- Date published: November 19, 2026
- The instructions cover open work permits for Start-up Business Class permanent residence applicants under the International Mobility Program
- The update includes new eligibility criteria and documentary evidence required as of December 19, 2025, clarification of the criteria and documents required for renewals, considerations when a designated entity is suspended, updates to out-of-date contact information, a new user remark stating the permit is not authorized for employment in Quebec, and more detailed instructions for assessing applications, including significant benefit considerations and rationale, clarification on dual intent, and clearer information about the letter of support and commitment certificate
- This update is to the department's operational instructions for officers and does not change program eligibility or points for applicants
