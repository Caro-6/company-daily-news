## Latest Announcements

### IRCC: Atlantic Immigration Program work permit (C18) instructions updated

On August 25, IRCC updated the Atlantic Immigration Program work permit (R204(c) – C18) program delivery instructions: clarifying renewal procedures, updating acceptable proof of permanent residence application submission, removing outdated Atlantic Immigration Pilot Program (AIPP) references, and adding officer-review details.

### IRCC: Super visa updated under new Ministerial Instructions

On August 25, IRCC updated the super visa program delivery instructions under new Ministerial Instructions: introducing two options to help hosts and applicants meet the Minimum Necessary Income requirement, with updated eligibility and assessment guidance. The Ministerial Instructions took effect on March 31, 2026 and apply to every super visa application processed on or after that date.
