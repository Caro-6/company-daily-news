# Canada Immigration Daily Briefing (September 29, 2026)

## Today's Highlights
- Two updates from official channels today: Express Entry held Round #446 for the Canadian Experience Class, issuing 2,000 invitations with a minimum CRS score of 518; and the federal government announced approximately $1.2 million in funding for three new projects promoting Francophone immigration in Newfoundland and Labrador, Saskatchewan, and Northern Ontario.

## Latest Announcements and Policy Changes
- Federal support for new Francophone immigration projects
- Announcement date: September 29, 2026 (St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador)
- Approximately $1.2 million in funding under the Francophone Immigration Support Program
- Project 1: Led by the Government of Newfoundland and Labrador — a sustainable corridor for Francophone and bilingual talent, with targeted overseas recruitment in education, health care, and early childhood education; $350,000 over 2 years
- Project 2: The Conseil économique et coopératif de la Saskatchewan's "Destination Saskatchewan" — a turnkey approach matching local labour market needs with international talent, targeting employers in agriculture, health care, mining, energy, technology, and hospitality; $569,000 over 2 years
- Project 3: Implementation of the second phase of the "Osez le Nord" coordinated promotional strategy developed by the Carrefour francophone de Sudbury, promoting the regionalization of Francophone immigration in Northern Ontario; $300,000 over 2 years
- Jointly announced by Minister of Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Lena Metlege Diab and Newfoundland and Labrador Minister of Jobs, Growth and Rural Development Lin Paddock
